Privacy Policy

Calm AM, Calm Teen AM, and CalmER ABC (iOS and Android) — Last updated October 2026

These apps are designed with privacy as a core principle.

Data Collection

These apps do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All information you enter, including check-in entries, student records, incident logs, and notes, is stored only on your own device using local storage. We have no access to it.

Accounts and Tracking

The apps do not require an account. They contain no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking.

Sharing and Export

Any export, backup, or sharing of information (such as saving a PDF chart, creating a backup file, or the "Share With My Trusted Adult" feature) is started manually and only by you. You decide where that information goes. Once you share it outside the app, it is no longer covered by this policy.

Student Information

CalmER ABC is a documentation tool for educators. You are responsible for entering and handling student information in line with your school's policies and applicable law. Because data stays on your device, protect it with your device's passcode or screen lock. Deleting the app, or using the in-app clear-data option, removes your data.

Children

Calm Teen AM is intended for ages 11 and up. No personal information is collected or sent to us from any of the apps.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the apps evolve. If a future version adds optional cloud sync, this page will be updated first and will explain what is stored and how to delete it.

Contact
